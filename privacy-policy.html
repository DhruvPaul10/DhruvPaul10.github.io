Letterbrook: Word Puzzle Game Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Developer: Tumbleforge Games (public developer name)

Letterbrook is a free word puzzle game supported by ads. Puzzles work offline and no account is needed. The game has no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The app stores puzzle progress, found words, settings and your coin balance in Android's private app storage, together with a backup copy of your progress. This game data is not sent to the developer: there is no developer-operated server, account, analytics or cloud service. Android may include app data in your device backups according to your backup settings.

Advertising

Letterbrook shows ads from Google AdMob: a video you can choose to watch for a free hint, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, such as your advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), information about your device, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. This information goes to Google, not to the developer. Google explains how it uses this information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and your choices

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. Where that applies, you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices (ads). On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google or privacy settings. You can turn off music, sounds and vibration in the game's Settings. To erase the game data stored on your device, use Android Settings > Apps > Letterbrook > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Data sharing and retention

The developer does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Advertising data is collected and kept by Google under Google's policies.

Children

Letterbrook is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app and posted here. For privacy questions, email tumbleforgegames@gmail.com.
